# 3.01 :: Jefferson's Presidency
## 3.01.01 :: Jefferson's Presidency (1801-1809)
### Overview
- Wrote the Declaration of Independence
- U.S. Minister to France
- First Secretary of State (Under George Washington)
- Adam's Vice President
- Led the Democratic-Republican Party
- Moments in his Presidency
	- **Inauguration**
		- First peaceful transfer of power between rival parties
	- **Louisiana Purchase (1803)**
	- Cut the National Debt
		- Reduced the Military
	- Repealed the Whiskey Tax
	- **Marbury v. Madison (1803)**
	- Embargo Act of 1807
		- Banned international trade
		- Hoped it would force the US to be independent
