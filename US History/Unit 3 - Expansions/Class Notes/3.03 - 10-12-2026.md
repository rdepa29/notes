## The "American System"
- ### Led by Henry Clay and John Quincy Adams in 1816
- ### Main Points
	- **High Tariffs** to protect American industries and generate revenue for US
		- Southerners felt this benefited northern manufacturers more
	- **Preserve the Bank of the US** to stabilize the currency
		- which was unable to be recharted in the 1830s
	- **Develop a system of internal improvements** which would knit the nation together
		- roads, canals, etc.
		- Ill-funded by Congress, although it did yield developments
