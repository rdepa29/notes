# 2.04 :: The Constitution
## 2.04.1 :: The Articles of Confederation (1781-1789)
### Overview & Terms
#### Terms
- confederation
	- a union of states united for purposes of common action
- article (in relation to [[#2.04.1 The Articles of Confederation (1781-1789)|The Articles of Confederation]])
	- a section in the *Articles of Confederation*
- Confederation Congress
	- First form of US government
	- No *national* executive or judicial branch
		- States still contain *local* executive and judicial branches
	- No *national* military
		- States still contain *local* militias
	- No *national* taxes
		- States still contain *local* taxes
	- Northwest Ordinance *(1787)*
		- Law that outlines the *requirements for areas to be considered states*
- Shays' Rebellion *(1787)*
	- First revolution against *the confederation*
	- *Daniel Shays* was a farmer in Massachusetts who rebelled against taxes
	- No *national* system that could *slow down the rebellion*
- [Shays' Rebellion Video (2:29)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lBWs9LpCg8I)
## 2.04.2 :: The Constitutional Convention (May-September 1787)
### Overview
- Convention called to *revise* [[#2.04.1 The Articles of Confederation (1781-1789)|The Articles of Confederations]] at **Independence Hall, Philadelphia**
- *James Madison* came ready with a blueprint for a national government with powers divided between three branches
	- Also known as the *Virginia Plan*
## 2.04.3 :: Constitutional Compromises
### Overview
- Increase size of *federal government (federalism)*
- Power of *federal government*
	- Three branches
	- Checks & Balances
- Representation
	- *Great Compromise* with bicameral legislature
	- *House of Representatives* based on state population
	- *Senate* has equal representation
		- 2 senators per state
- Slavery & The $\frac{3}{5}$ Compromise
	- Slaves aren't citizens, counted as property
	- Only count towards *House of Representatives*
- Electoral College
	- Actually elects the new *Executive Branch*
