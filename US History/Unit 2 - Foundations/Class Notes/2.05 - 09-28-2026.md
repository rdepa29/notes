# 2.05 :: Washington's Presidency
## 2.05.1 :: Ratification of the Constitution
### Ratification
- 9 out of 13 states needed to *ratify* the constitution
- Each state held a *convention* to vote on ratification
- Federalists and Anti-Federalists factions campaigned for and against it
	- *The Federalist Papers* were published to convince New Yorkers to vote to ratify

## 2.05.2 :: Washington's Presidency
### Washington's Presidency
- ##### [Washington's Presidency Video (4:52)](https://www.youtubeeducation.com/watch?v=LO0mlLlKa2I)
	- If Washington failed, the country would go down with him
	- Washington was the first for a lot of stuff:
		- The concept of a president was not ever used before the US
	- Before the first *State of the Union address*, Washington marked up a copy of the constitution, showing his dedication to the constitution
	- Washington wasn't thinking about the next few years, he was thinking about the next century for the country
- Had *no desire* to serve as president
- Created the **Cabinet** of officials to advise him
	- Included *Thomas Jefferson* (Democratic-Republican) and *Alexander Hamilton* (Federalist)
- *Whiskey Rebellion* in Pennsylvania
- *Neutrality Proclamation* with French Revolution
- *Farewell Address* when he left office
