## 2.03.2 :: The War
#### First Half: Declaration of Independence to Saratoga
- Timeline
	- August-September 1776
		- Washington's army is defeated at New York and is forced to retreat
	- December 26th, 1776
		- Washington crosses the Delaware for a surprise attack on Trenton
	- September 26th, 1777
		- British capture Philadelphia
	- October 4th, 1777
		- Washington fails at a counterattack, but impresses European observers
	- October 17th, 1777
		- British defeat at Battle of Saratoga, convinces France to join America
	- December 1777-June 1778
		- Valley Force
	- February 1778
		- French Alliance formalized
#### Second Half: French Alliance to Treaty of Paris
- Timeline
	- 1778
		- France joins the War
	- 1778-1781
		- Britain shifts strategy to the south, but faces growing American resistance
	- 1780
		- Washington's most trusted general, Benedict Arnold, defects to the British
	- 1781
		- Cornwallis surrenders at Yorktown
		- French Navy supports and makes it possible
	- 1783
		- Britain officially recognizes America as an independent state
