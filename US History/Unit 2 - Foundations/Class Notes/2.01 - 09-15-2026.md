# 2.01 :: Road to Revolution
## 2.01.1 :: Revolutionary Influences
### Enlightenment
- Emphasized reason and logic should guide society & government, not tradition or religion
- Prior to this, most European governments were monarchies
	- Power was a given by God
	- Questioning power was questioning God.
- Natural rights belong to all people
	- John Locke
- Government power should be divided and limited
	- Baron de Montesquieu
- These ideas shaped the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution
### First Great Awakening
- Wave of religious revival that swept the colonies in the 1730s-1740s
- Emphasized personal, emotional connection to God over formal church
- Preachers like Jonathan Edwards and George Whitefield drew massive crowds
- Challenged traditional church authority and encouraged colonists to think independently
### French & Indian War
- Even though the British won, they accumulated a massive debt that they could not pay off
- Proclamation Line of 1763
	- Followed Appalachian Mountains
	- Prevented colonists from settling west of the thirteen colonies; separated colonists from Native Americans
	- Did not work
		- British settlers believed they had a right to expand after they helped win the war that granted the British that land
	- **[Fast Facts on the Proclamation of 1763 :: 3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HKNTBHmWOyA)**
		- Pontiac
		- Many colonists were already out west
		- Most colonists ignored it
		- George Washington believed that the line wouldn't last
			- Eventually bought the land
		- All it really did was create hatred for the British from all parties
