# Kinetic Molecular Theory
#### Theory
- Not a guess
- Similar to an axiom or a proof in math; proven and used as fact
## Overview
All matter is made of particles (ions, atoms, molecules) that are constantly moving
## Phase Energies
- #### KE
	- Kinetic Energy
	- Motion
- #### PE
	- Potential Energy
	- Bonds/Position
	- Strong bonds -> Lower *PE*
	- Weaker/no bonds -> Higher *PE*
### Solids
- Fixed shape
- *Can't* be compressed
- Motion usually consists of vibrations/oscillations
- Lowest *KE* & *PE*
### Liquids
- Not fixed shape
- *Can't* be compressed
- Still close together
- Moderate *KE* & *PE*
### Gas
- Not fixed shape
- Fits container
- *Can* be compressed
- Highest *KE* & *PE*
## Phase Change Diagram
During Phase Changes, the temperature of the substance stays the same
### Temperature (T, t) vs. Heat (Q, q)
- Heat is a type of energy (*thermal energy*)
	- An energy *transferred* between substances and systems
	- Flows from higher temperature to lower temperature
	- *Quantifiable* (Unit: *J*)
		- Can't be measured
	- Amount (*Q*) is mass dependent
- Temperature is a property
	- Description of a substance
	- Average *KE*
	- *Measured* in Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
	- Value is mass independent
